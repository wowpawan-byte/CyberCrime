# Meadow

A little clarity. Before you buy.

## Identity

Meadow is a calm, approachable name for a first-pass shopping-site check. The lowercase wordmark softens the technical subject. The rounded “m” mark accompanies the name in the interface. The toolbar check symbol remains familiar. These are project identity choices, not a trademark clearance or claim of name ownership.

## Type

- **Rajdhani SemiBold:** wordmark, verdict headings, large slide titles. Use it at sizes where its character stays readable.
- **Inter Regular/Medium:** evidence, explanations, settings and slide body copy. Its role is comfortable reading, particularly inside the narrow popup.
- Product: body 14px / 1.55, evidence labels 13px, expanded evidence 12px / 1.6, verdict 34px, wordmark 30px.
- Slides: large Rajdhani headlines, body approximately 25–29px on a 1280 × 720 canvas. Avoid long paragraphs.
- Fonts are bundled locally. The extension does not request fonts from Google during browsing. Both families use the SIL Open Font License; the original license files are in `fonts`.

## Palette

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Charcoal canvas | #191A1B |
| Panel | #222325 |
| Raised surface | #2B2C2E |
| Quiet border | #363739 |
| Primary off-white | #EEEEE9 |
| Secondary gray | #ADAEAA |

Keep warning states monochrome. Distinguish them through the written verdict, a different symbol and contrast. Never make color the only cue. High Risk uses an off-white filled warning symbol; Unknown uses a question mark/dashed treatment. “Checked” describes a performed check, not certified safety.

## Shape and space

Rounded panels in the product, 18px radius, generous 24px main margins. Keep the slide canvas mostly open: one idea, one image or typographic emphasis. Avoid dashboard-style slide grids and dense architecture diagrams. The screenshots are product evidence, not decorative mockups.

## Voice

Specific, calm, direct. Tell the person what was observed and what they can do next.

- “Sensitive fields are displayed on an HTTP page.”
- “Pause before entering personal or payment details.”
- “Registration information is unavailable.”

Avoid “100% safe,” “scam confirmed,” “stops every scam,” “revolutionary AI” and unsupported accuracy claims. The project does not block transactions or investigate merchant fulfilment.

## Illustration and imagery

Use ordinary shopping objects in grayscale, with restrained detail and plenty of empty space. The existing backpack illustration in the demo is the sample treatment, visible in `assets/storefront-before.png` and the visual brand sheet. It makes the scenario approachable without adding fear-based imagery. Keep it separate from evidence of risk: a product illustration cannot prove trustworthiness.

Use genuine screenshots of this build for product claims. Label local simulations. Do not substitute a fabricated warning screen or generic hacker/lock imagery. No image-generation model or external stock artwork was used for this package.

## Product description

“Meadow is a browser extension that runs seven focused checks on an unfamiliar store and explains visible warning signs before you enter details.”

## Scope of the rebrand

Version 1.1 changes the name, typography and presentation package. Existing detection rules remain unchanged. The internal `shopcheck` folder names and message/data identifiers intentionally remain compatible with the tested build and the existing unpacked-extension paths.
